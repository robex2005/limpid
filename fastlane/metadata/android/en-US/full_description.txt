On some Samsung phones running One UI, apps like Facebook play videos with a gray, washed-out haze. The picture becomes correct the moment any system overlay appears (for example the volume panel). The known workaround is <i>Developer options → Disable HW overlays</i>, but that switch resets on every reboot and costs battery when left on all the time.

<b>Limpid applies that switch for you:</b>
<ul>
<li><b>Enable globally</b> – HW overlays disabled everywhere until reboot.</li>
<li><b>Only for selected apps</b> – turns on automatically when you open an app from your list, and off when you leave it.</li>
<li><b>Disable</b> – back to normal system behavior.</li>
</ul>

<b>Requires Shizuku</b> (no root needed). Limpid sends the same SurfaceFlinger call the Developer options switch makes, through Shizuku, and has no background service of its own. After a reboot, Shizuku wakes Limpid and the saved mode is applied again.

<b>Privacy:</b> no internet permission, no analytics, no ads. The accessibility service is used only to know which app is in the foreground (for the per-app mode); it never reads screen content.

Available in English and Romanian.
